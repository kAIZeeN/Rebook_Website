About Us

ReBook is a community of passionate readers striving for the advancement of accessible education. Through book donations, monetary contributions, and our digital archive, we facilitate the spread of knowledge one reader at a time. The platform strives to foster personal growth and shared humanity through community collaboration. Many unused books sit idle while many students struggle to find the learning materials they need. That’s why ReBook makes it our goal to give forgotten books a new purpose and empower our community.

Our Mission

ReBook aims to provide an accessible platform for donating books and financial support while preserving donated books through an online archive. It promotes book sharing and makes learning resources more accessible.

Our Vision

To build a community where books are shared, preserved, and made accessible to everyone through a digital platform.

Objectives

To develop a web-based system that allows users to donate books or money and enables administrators to manage these donations. The system will also provide an online archive where users can browse and access information about donated books.

Why it Matters

A book that sits unused on a shelf helps no one. By donating, sharing, and archiving books, ReBook keeps learning materials in circulation so students, teachers, and readers can find what they need.
