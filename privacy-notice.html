Privacy Notice

ReBook is a front-end website demo. This notice explains what happens when you enter information into its forms.

Information you enter

The Contact Us form does not send information to a server or save it in a database. Submitting it only checks the required fields in your browser and displays a confirmation; it does not deliver your message to ReBook.

Third-party resources

Some pages load fonts or icons from external providers, including Google Fonts and the Font Awesome CDN. Your browser may connect to those providers to load these files. ReBook does not control their handling of connection data.

Questions

For this project demo, contact details shown on the Contact Us page are placeholders. Do not enter sensitive personal information into the forms.
